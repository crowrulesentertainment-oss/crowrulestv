About PNWM

A community-driven network built around real mothers, real stories, and real connections.

Pacific Northwest Mothers Worldwide is a community-driven initiative supporting mothers through connection, storytelling, education, and real-life resources.


We believe empowered mothers build stronger families, stronger neighborhoods, and stronger communities across Washington, the Pacific Northwest, and beyond.

// CORE MISSION

Our Mission

Three core principles powering the PNWM network.

NODE_01

CONNECTION

Creating safe spaces where mothers can connect, share experiences, exchange ideas, and discover that they are not alone.

NODE_02

SUPPORT

Connecting mothers with emotional, practical, educational, and community-based resources.

NODE_03

GROWTH

Helping mothers build confidence, skills, opportunities, and stronger futures for themselves and their families.
