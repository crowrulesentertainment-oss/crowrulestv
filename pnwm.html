About PNWM

A community network built around real mothers, real stories, and real connections.

Pacific Northwest Mothers Worldwide is a community-driven initiative created to support mothers through connection, storytelling, education, resources, and community.

We believe mothers deserve spaces where they can be heard, supported, encouraged, and connected with others who understand the realities of family life.

PNWM begins in the Pacific Northwest and looks outward toward a worldwide community.

What We Stand For

Three principles form the foundation of the PNWM network.

NODE_01 // CONNECTION

Connection

Creating welcoming spaces where mothers can connect, share experiences, exchange ideas, and discover that they are not alone.

NODE_02 // SUPPORT

Support

Connecting mothers with practical, emotional, educational, and community-based resources.

NODE_03 // GROWTH

Growth

Helping mothers build confidence, skills, opportunities, and stronger futures for themselves and their families.
