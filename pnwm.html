About PNWM

Pacific Northwest Mothers Worldwide is a community-driven initiative supporting mothers through connection, storytelling, education, and real-life resources. We believe empowered mothers build stronger communities across Washington and beyond.

Our Mission

Connection

Creating safe spaces for mothers to share stories and experiences.

Support

Providing emotional, practical, and community-based support systems.

Growth

Helping mothers build confidence, skills, and opportunities.

Programs & Initiatives

Talk Radio Voices

Anonymous voicemails and real stories from mothers across Tacoma and the Pacific Northwest.

Coffee Between The Storms

Community meetups focused on healing conversations and connection.

Video Yearbooks

Capturing school and family stories through documentary-style media.
