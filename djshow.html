The Beginning

What Was the D&J Show?

D&J was a project built by two friends. It wasn't about having a giant production studio or a massive audience. It was about creating something together and having a place to talk, laugh, experiment and make memories.

🎥

What We Made

The D&J Show was a creative video project centered around Daniel and Jonathan. It gave us a reason to turn conversations and ideas into something other people could experience.

🖤

Why We Did It

Because making something together was fun. The show didn't need to be perfect. It needed to be ours.

🐦

Where It Lives Now

Remembering Daniel Meeker

Some projects end. The memories don't.

Daniel was part of the reason the D&J Show existed in the first place. He wasn't simply a person who appeared in an old video. He was part of the story.

This section is intentionally about remembering him as a friend, preserving the good memories and recognizing the part he played in the creative journey that eventually became part of CrowRules Entertainment.
