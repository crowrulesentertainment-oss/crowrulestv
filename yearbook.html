The Vision

Why CrowRules Yearbook?

Classic yearbooks remember a school year. CrowRules Yearbook remembers a creative community.

“Classic yearbooks capture a school year. CrowRules Yearbook captures the journey.”

Not Just A School Year

CrowRules Yearbook can document creators, community members, entertainment projects, shows, events, milestones and moments from an entire creative ecosystem.

A Living Archive

Each edition becomes part of a growing CrowRules historical archive rather than disappearing when the year ends.

People First

The heart of the Yearbook is the people. Members, creators, collaborators and supporters can become part of the story.

Built For The Future

Digital editions allow CrowRules to preserve video, links, creator channels, show history, photographs and interactive memories.
