01 — The Story

What Talk Radio was

A place to talk

Talk Radio was built around conversation. It was a way to put thoughts, opinions, stories, local subjects and everyday life into a show format instead of keeping everything in my head.

An experiment

It was also an experiment in becoming a creator: learning how to record, edit, publish, build an audience and slowly turn an idea into something real.

The bridge

Talk Radio became part of the creative path that eventually led toward Tacoma Nights — a more personal, focused and documentary-style version of the same desire to tell stories.

02 — The Reason

Why I did it

The point was never simply to make another internet show. It was to create a place where I could speak, experiment and build something of my own.

01

Find my voice

02

Build something

03

Connect with people

Every version of the show taught me something that could be carried into whatever came next.

04 — Looking Back / Looking Forward

Could Talk Radio come back?

Maybe — but it does not have to return exactly as it was. The best parts of Talk Radio can live on in newer formats: conversation, local stories, commentary, community and the freedom to talk about whatever is happening.

Instead of competing with Tacoma Nights, a revived Talk Radio could become a special archive series, occasional podcast/video special, interview format, or an experimental late-night show inside the CrowRules universe.
