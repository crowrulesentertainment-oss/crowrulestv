Stop Waiting For Perfect
PRODUCTION
▼

One of the easiest traps for a creator is waiting until everything is perfect.

Better camera. Better lighting. Better set. Better website. Better microphone.

But eventually the equipment has to stop being the excuse.

Make the thing. Learn from the thing. Make the next thing better.
