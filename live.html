Custom HLS Live

CrowRules+ supports direct HLS live streams generated by your own streaming infrastructure. Cameras, Streamlabs, OBS or other encoders can send RTMP/SRT to your streaming server, which publishes an HLS .m3u8 stream for the CrowRules+ universal player.

01 · SOURCE Camera · S24 Ultra · OBS · Streamlabs
02 · INGEST RTMP or SRT
03 · STREAM HLS .m3u8
04 · CROW RULES+ Universal Live Player
